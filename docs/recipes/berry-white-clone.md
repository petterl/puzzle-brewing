# Berry White clone

Raspberry and white chocolate melomel, oaked and back-sweetened.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | When |
|------------|--------|------|
| Honey (wildflower if possible) | 3.3 kg | Primary |
| Water | to 10 L | Primary |
| Lalvin 71B-1122 | 1 packet | Primary |
| Fermaid-O | ~8 g total | TOSNA, four doses |
| Pektolase | per packet | Primary |
| Frozen raspberries | ~2 kg | Secondary |
| American oak chips, medium toast | to taste | Secondary, 1–2 weeks, taste every few days |
| Potassium metabisulfite | per label | Stabilising |
| Potassium sorbate | per label | Stabilising |
| White chocolate flavouring | bench test | Before bottling |
| Lactose (optional) | bench test | Before bottling |

## Nutrients (TOSNA, Fermaid-O only)

About 2 g per dose at 24 h, 48 h, 72 h and at the one-third sugar break (≈ SG 1.071) or day 7, whichever comes first. Check the totals in a TOSNA calculator before brewing.

## Process

1. Sanitise everything.
2. Dissolve the honey in water to 10 L and check OG (target ≈ 1.107).
3. Pitch 71B at around 18–20 °C.
4. Dose Fermaid-O per the schedule; degas gently before each dose.
5. Ferment dry (≈ 0.998), then rack onto raspberries.
6. Oak with chips, tasting every few days.
7. Stabilise with metabisulfite and sorbate.
8. Back-sweeten to FG 1.020–1.028.
9. Bench test the white chocolate flavouring, then bottle.
